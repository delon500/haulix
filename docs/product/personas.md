# Personas

Five personas guide every feature. User stories in the [feature catalogue](feature-catalogue/README.md) are written from their point of view.

| Persona | Who they are | What they care about | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maya | University student | Lectures and study material offline, limited storage, saving time | Consumer features |
| Diego | Video creator and editor | Source footage, high quality, large files, batch downloads, automation | Consumer features |
| Priya | Remote worker who travels | Unreliable or metered connections, downloads that survive interruptions, data costs | Consumer features |
| Sam | Power user who runs a home server | Control, automation, privacy, self-hosting, keyboard-driven workflows | Consumer and developer features |
| Lerato | IT manager at a 200-person media company | Central control, security policies, easy rollout to staff | Business features (v4.0) |

A few stories use other users where a persona doesn't fit, such as a screen-reader user or a user who reads a right-to-left language.
