# Feature Catalogue — v1.0 Launch

v1.0 is the public launch: 15 features (F-059–F-073) that ship the product's main differentiators and start earning revenue. The headline features are searching inside saved videos, and sending links from a phone to download at home. The format, personas and tags are the same as the earlier files.

## Search and AI

These three features work together to make saved video and audio searchable by what is said in them, which no mainstream download manager offers. Everything runs on the user's own computer.

### F-059 Local transcription (Whisper) ★

**Search and AI · v1.0 · Pro · ★ Differentiator**

**What it is:** The app turns speech in downloaded videos and audio into text, using Whisper, an open-source speech recognition model that runs on the user's own computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The transcript is saved with the file, with a timestamp for every line.

**User story:** As Maya, I want written transcripts of my lectures, so that I can skim, search and quote them instead of rewatching hours of video.

**Scenario:** Maya downloads a 90-minute lecture. While she makes dinner, the app transcribes it in the background. Afterwards she opens the transcript next to the video and reads the section she missed in class.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Transcribes video and audio in at least 20 languages, detecting the language automatically.
- Runs entirely offline; no audio or text leaves the computer.
- Uses the graphics card when available and falls back to the processor; a 1-hour file takes under 15 minutes on a typical modern laptop.
- Transcription runs in a background queue, at low priority, and pauses when the computer is on battery if the user chooses.
- Transcripts can be viewed beside the file and exported as .txt, .srt or .vtt.
- Free users get a limited version; the exact limits are set with pricing before v1.0.

### F-060 Full-text search across transcripts and documents ★

**Search and AI · v1.0 · Pro · ★ Differentiator**

**What it is:** One search box that searches every transcript, plus the text inside downloaded documents like PDFs and Word files. Results show the matching words in context, so users find what they need without remembering which file it was in.

**User story:** As Maya, I want to search everything I've downloaded for a topic, so that I can find every lecture and reading that mentions it before an exam.

**Scenario:** Maya types "photosynthesis light reactions". The app finds matches in 4 lecture transcripts and 2 PDFs, each with the sentence where it appears, ranked by relevance.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Searches transcripts and the text in PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown and EPUB files in the library.
- Results appear as the user types, within 300 ms for a library of 1,000 hours of transcripts.
- Each result shows the file, a snippet with the matching words highlighted, and the timestamp or page.
- Supports exact phrases in quotes, and filters by file type, date and source site.
- The search index is stored locally and updates automatically as files are added or removed.

### F-061 Jump to the moment in a video from search ★

**Search and AI · v1.0 · Pro · ★ Differentiator**

**What it is:** Clicking a search result from a transcript opens the video in the built-in player at the exact moment those words are spoken.

**User story:** As Diego, I want to jump straight to the point in an interview where a topic comes up, so that I can find the clip I need in seconds instead of scrubbing through hours of footage.

**Scenario:** Diego searches his downloaded interviews for "first camera". He clicks a result and the player opens at 34:12, just before the speaker begins telling the story he wants to clip.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Clicking a transcript search result opens the built-in player at that moment, starting 3 seconds early for context.
- The transcript scrolls alongside playback, highlighting the current line.
- Clicking any line in the transcript jumps the video to that point.
- From that point, users can create a clip (feature F-008) or copy a link with the timestamp.

## Mobile and remote

These four features connect a phone to the desktop app for the first time. The phone doesn't download anything itself: it sends links to the user's computer and reports progress.

### F-062 Mobile share-target and remote app

**Multi-device and remote · v1.0 · Free**

**What it is:** A small companion app for Android and iOS. It appears in the phone's Share menu, so any link can be sent to the desktop app, and it shows what's downloading at home. Users pair it with their computer once by scanning a QR code.

**User story:** As Priya, I want to see my home downloads on my phone, so that I know whether a big file is ready before I get home.

**Scenario:** Priya scans the QR code shown in the desktop app's settings to pair her phone. Later, on the train, she opens the companion app and sees her 8 GB download is at 85% with 4 minutes left.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Available on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store).
- Pairing takes one QR-code scan and under 30 seconds.
- Shows active, queued and finished downloads with progress, speed and time left.
- Users can pause, resume or cancel downloads from the phone.
- The app appears in the phone's Share menu for links.
- Works with one or more paired computers.

### F-063 Send from phone, download at home ★

**Multi-device and remote · v1.0 · Pro · ★ Differentiator**

**What it is:** From anywhere, users share a link from their phone, and their computer at home downloads it, using its faster, cheaper or uncapped connection. The file is ready when they get home, without using phone data or storage.

**User story:** As Priya, I want to send a video link from my phone to my home computer, so that it downloads on my home internet instead of my mobile data.

**Scenario:** At lunch, Priya sees a 2 GB course video linked in a newsletter. She taps Share, then the app, then "Download at home". Her home PC downloads it on fibre within minutes, and her phone shows a notification when it's done.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Links shared from the phone reach the home computer within 5 seconds when it's online.
- If the computer is off or asleep, links wait securely and start when it next comes online.
- Users can choose quality, queue and folder from the phone, or use defaults and rules.
- Works across networks (mobile data, other Wi-Fi), not only on the home network.
- Links pass through our servers encrypted end to end (feature F-064); our servers can't read them.
- Free users get a limited version; the exact limits are set with pricing before v1.0.

### F-064 End-to-end encrypted sync and relay

**Security and privacy · v1.0 · Pro**

**What it is:** Links and download status passing between the phone and the computer go through our relay server, encrypted so that only the user's own devices can read them. We can't see what users download, and neither can anyone who breaks into our servers.

**User story:** As Sam, I want everything that passes through the company's servers encrypted on my devices, so that my download activity stays private even from the app makers.

**Scenario:** Sam sends a link from his phone. It's encrypted on the phone, passes through the relay as unreadable data, and is decrypted only by his home server. The relay operator sees only that an encrypted message passed between two devices.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- All messages between a user's devices are encrypted on the sending device and decrypted only on the receiving device.
- Encryption keys are created during pairing and never leave the user's devices.
- Uses an established, well-reviewed encryption library and protocol, not a custom design.
- The relay stores encrypted messages only until delivered, or for at most 7 days.
- An independent security review is completed before launch, with findings published.

### F-065 Mobile push notifications

**Notifications · v1.0 · Free**

**What it is:** The phone app sends notifications when downloads at home finish, fail or need attention, so users know without opening the app.

**User story:** As Diego, I want a notification on my phone when large footage downloads finish, so that I can head back to my desk only when there's something to work on.

**Scenario:** Diego is in a client meeting while 40 GB downloads at the studio. His phone buzzes: "4 downloads finished · 1 needs attention (login expired)." He taps it to see which one.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Notifications for finished downloads, failed downloads, and downloads needing input.
- Several events close together are grouped into one notification.
- Each notification type can be turned on or off from the phone.
- Notification content is encrypted end to end; the push service sees only that a notification was sent.
- Tapping a notification opens the relevant download in the phone app.

## Library

These three features turn the downloads list into a library people browse, watch and organise, not just a record of files.

### F-066 Tags, collections and favourites

**Library management · v1.0 · Free**

**What it is:** Users can add tags to downloads, group them into collections (like playlists that can hold any file type), and mark favourites. One file can have many tags and belong to several collections, without being copied or moved.

**User story:** As Diego, I want to tag footage by project and theme, so that I can pull together every clip for a new edit without searching folder by folder.

**Scenario:** Diego tags clips "drone", "harbour" and "client-Acme". Starting a new Acme edit, he opens the "client-Acme" tag and filters by "drone", which gives him 14 clips from across 3 months of downloads.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Downloads can have any number of tags; tags auto-complete as the user types.
- Collections hold any mix of file types and can be sorted manually.
- Favourites can be toggled with one click or a keyboard shortcut.
- The library can be filtered by tags (with AND / OR), collections and favourites.
- Rules (feature F-043) can add tags automatically.
- Tags and collections don't change where files are stored on disk.

### F-067 Built-in media player and previews

**Library management · v1.0 · Free**

**What it is:** Downloaded video and audio play inside the app, and documents and images can be previewed, without opening another program. The player is also where transcripts and jump-to-moment (features F-059–F-061) appear.

**User story:** As Maya, I want to watch lectures inside the app next to their transcript, so that I can study in one window.

**Scenario:** Maya double-clicks a lecture. It plays in the app with the transcript beside it. She speeds playback to 1.5x and turns on the embedded subtitles.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Plays the common video and audio formats the app produces (MP4, MKV, WebM, MP3, M4A, Opus).
- Controls: play/pause, seek, volume, speed (0.5x–3x), subtitles on/off, full screen, picture-in-picture.
- Shows chapters from the file's metadata, and the transcript side by side when one exists.
- Remembers where the user stopped each file and offers to resume.
- Quick-look previews for images, PDFs and text files with the Space key.
- Files can still be opened in the user's preferred external player.

### F-068 Watched/unwatched and progress tracking

**Library management · v1.0 · Free**

**What it is:** The library shows which videos and audio files the user has finished, started or not yet opened, with a progress bar on each one, much like a streaming service.

**User story:** As Priya, I want to see which talks I've finished and which I've only started, so that I know what's left to listen to on my next trip.

**Scenario:** Priya opens her "Conference talks" collection. Five show a full bar and a tick, two show partial progress bars, and eight are marked as new.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Each media file shows as New, In progress (with a progress bar), or Watched.
- Playing past 90% marks a file as watched automatically.
- Users can mark items watched or unwatched manually, individually or in bulk.
- The library can be filtered to show only unwatched or in-progress items.
- Progress syncs to the phone app through the encrypted relay (feature F-064).

## Accounts and pricing

These two features are how the product earns money. Everything free keeps working without an account; an account is only needed for Pro and for pairing a phone.

### F-069 Accounts and Pro tier

**Monetization · v1.0 · Business model**

**What it is:** Users can create an account and upgrade to Pro by monthly or yearly subscription. Pro removes the limits on free features (rules, subscriptions, transcription, send-from-phone) and unlocks Pro-only features. Payment is handled by a merchant-of-record service, which takes care of sales tax and VAT in every country.

**User story:** As Maya, I want to try Pro features for free with limits before paying, so that I only upgrade once I know they're worth it to me.

**Scenario:** Maya uses up her free transcription allowance in the first week of exams. The app shows how much she has used and an "Upgrade to Pro" option with a 14-day free trial. She starts the trial, and her limits lift immediately.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- All free features work without an account; sign-up is only asked for when the user chooses a Pro feature or pairs a phone.
- Sign-up by email or by Google, Apple or Microsoft login.
- Monthly and yearly plans, with prices shown in the user's local currency and tax included where required.
- A 14-day free trial of Pro, with no payment details needed to start it.
- Each limited feature shows usage so far (for example, "3 hours of transcription used this month").
- Users can cancel in two clicks inside the app; Pro features stay until the end of the paid period.
- Downgrading never deletes data: rules, subscriptions and transcripts beyond free limits are kept, just paused.

### F-070 One-time licence option

**Monetization · v1.0 · Business model**

**What it is:** For people who dislike subscriptions, Pro can also be bought once, as a lifetime licence for the desktop features (like IDM's model). Cloud features that cost us money every month, such as send-from-phone, need a subscription or a cheaper cloud add-on.

**User story:** As Sam, I want to buy the app once instead of subscribing, so that I own it and never pay monthly for software that runs on my own computer.

**Scenario:** Sam buys a lifetime licence. He enters the key on his desktop and laptop, and all Pro desktop features unlock permanently. He doesn't need send-from-phone, because he reaches his home server another way.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- A lifetime licence unlocks all Pro desktop features permanently, including future updates within the current major version.
- Each licence can be activated on up to 3 computers, managed from the user's account page.
- Activation works without an account, using a licence key; it checks online once, then works offline.
- The pricing page explains clearly which features need an ongoing subscription and why.
- Owners get a discounted upgrade when a new major version is released.

## Safety, onboarding and languages

These three features prepare the app for a much larger, less technical audience at launch.

### F-071 Malware hash checks for executables

**Security and privacy · v1.0 · Free**

**What it is:** When a user downloads a program or app installer (.exe, .msi, .dmg, .apk and similar), the app checks the file's fingerprint against a malware database such as VirusTotal and warns the user if it's known to be harmful. Only the fingerprint is sent, never the file itself.

**User story:** As Maya, I want a warning if a program I download is known malware, so that I don't infect my laptop by accident.

**Scenario:** Maya downloads a "free video converter" from an unfamiliar site. Before she opens it, the app shows a red warning: "38 security vendors flagged this file as malware." She deletes it.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Checks run automatically on completed downloads of executable and installer file types.
- Only the file's SHA-256 fingerprint is sent for lookup; the file never leaves the computer.
- Results show as Safe (no detections), Unknown (never seen before), or Warning (with the number of detections).
- Flagged files are never opened automatically, and opening one requires a confirmation.
- The check can be turned off in settings; it's described in the privacy policy.
- If the lookup service is unavailable, the download completes normally and shows "Not checked".

### F-072 Onboarding with sensible defaults

**UX and UI · v1.0 · Free**

**What it is:** A short first-run setup that gets new users downloading in under two minutes. It sets up the browser extension, asks a few simple questions (where to save files, simple or power-user view, preferred quality), and chooses good defaults for everything else.

**User story:** As Maya, I want the app set up for me in a couple of steps, so that I can start downloading without learning any settings.

**Scenario:** Maya installs the app. A welcome screen asks three questions, offers the Chrome extension with one click, and then suggests "Try it: paste any video link." Ninety seconds after installing, her first lecture is downloading.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- First-run setup takes 5 screens or fewer and can be skipped at any point.
- It detects installed browsers and offers the matching extension.
- Defaults (folder, quality, organisation) are sensible enough that most users never need to change them.
- In testing, at least 80% of first-time users complete a download within 2 minutes of opening the app.
- Setup can be run again from the Help menu.

### F-073 Right-to-left language support

**Accessibility and localization · v1.0 · Free**

**What it is:** The interface works correctly in languages written right to left, such as Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu, with the whole layout mirrored the way those readers expect.

**User story:** As a user in Egypt who reads Arabic, I want the app laid out right to left, so that it reads as naturally as any other app on my computer.

**Scenario:** A user switches the app's language to Arabic. Menus, lists and buttons flip to read right to left, progress bars fill from right to left, and filenames in English still display correctly inside the Arabic layout.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Selecting an RTL language mirrors the entire layout: navigation, lists, icons with direction, and progress bars.
- Mixed text (an English filename in an Arabic sentence, for example) displays in the correct order.
- Arabic and Hebrew translations are at least 90% complete at launch.
- RTL layouts are checked in automated screenshot tests before each release.
